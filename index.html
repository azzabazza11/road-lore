Passenger Tales

A passenger companion. Nearby places, read aloud as a short local sketch — not a map, not a navigator, and not something to operate while driving.

What to expect

  1. Allow location when asked.
  2. A first story can begin where you are, even before you move.
  3. Skip, Replay, and More are for the passenger. Stories start often; you can ease the pace later.
  4. Gemini voice and AI stories are available where the service is running. The on-device voice works offline.
A word on the telling. Narration may use a generated voice, drawing on local history and the open web. Names are sometimes mis-said, and like any AI-assisted sketch, a detail may be off. Take it as a companionable introduction, not a finished history.
